FAQ

Questions shop owners ask

If your question isn't here, message us on WhatsApp.

Do I need both the computer and the phone?

No. Work on the phone alone, the computer alone, or both together so the data stays in sync.

What if the shop's internet goes down?

The computer program keeps working offline, then syncs everything with the phone as soon as the connection is back.

Does it work with a fingerprint device?

Yes. It reads the punches from the device and works out attendance, lateness, overtime and lunch breaks, then payroll, automatically.

Can I work in dollars instead of lira?

Yes. Choose the app currency in Settings; other currencies stay available for invoices and vouchers at their rates.

Where is my data kept, and is it safe?

On Amazon Web Services servers, in a data centre in Ireland, inside the European Union. The connection between your device and the server is encrypted, and each account's data is kept apart: only you and the staff you add can see it. An account opens on one phone and one computer at a time, and the computer program also saves a daily backup of your data to the cloud.

Can my staff use the program?

Yes. You give each employee a user name and password and choose which sections they may see or change; or an employee can see only his own pay and attendance on his phone. Each extra account is $20 a year.

What if I forget my password?

Tap «Forgot password» on the sign-in screen and a code arrives by email so you can choose a new one. You can also sign in with Google or Apple.
